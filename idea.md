STEM Spark
Tagline: “From Science Concepts to Real-World STEM Solutions.”

STEM Lesson Planner AI is an agentic AI tool that helps teachers transform a science concept into an innovative, problem-based STEM lesson.
 How It Works

Input: Science concept — Conductors & Insulators

1. AI Concept Agent 
Analyzes the concept and suggests relevant real-life problems.

Example Problem:
How can we choose the right materials to make an electrical device safe and prevent electric shocks?

2. Teacher Selection 
The teacher reviews the AI-generated problems and selects the most relevant problem.

3. STEM Agent 
AI connects the problem with:

Science: Conductors and insulators
Technology: Testing tools/electrical circuits
Mathematics: Measuring and comparing results
Engineering: Designing a safe insulated electrical solution

4. STS–EDP Agent 
Builds the lesson around Science–Technology–Society and Engineering Design Process:
Ask → Imagine → Plan → Create → Test → Improve → Share

5. Lesson Plan Agent
Generates a complete teacher-ready lesson plan including objectives, activities, materials, STEM integration, HOTS questions, assessment, problem-solving task, and expected solution.

Teacher Benefit

The app reduces lesson-planning time and helps teachers innovate beyond textbook teaching, turning science concepts into meaningful real-world STEM challenges.

Concept → Real Problem → STEM Integration → EDP → Innovative Lesson Plan
